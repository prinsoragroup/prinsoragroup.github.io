The system prorates salary using the method configured for that company (calendar days, Monday–Friday working days, or fixed 30-day month) and estimates PAYE using standard progressive bands incorporating statutory CRA. It supports employee pension, optional NHF and NHIS, life insurance, qualifying mortgage interest, rent relief, employer pension, and employer-funded NSITF. Confirm eligibility and actual remittances; review all figures before approval. NSITF is employer-funded and is not deducted from employee net pay.
