Prinsora HR Consultancy Services

Prinsora HR Consultancy Services provides professional recruitment, HR outsourcing, workforce management, training, performance management, payroll support and business consulting solutions for organizations across Nigeria and beyond.

Recruitment & Executive Search

We recruit qualified candidates for entry-level, mid-level and executive positions across multiple industries. We also conduct candidate screening, interviews, background checks and reference verification.

HR Outsourcing

We manage employee records, onboarding, contracts, leave administration, disciplinary processes, payroll support and complete HR operations for businesses.

Training & Development

We organize staff orientation, leadership development, customer service training, compliance training and professional development programmes.

Business Consulting

We help startups and established businesses develop HR policies, organizational structures, standard operating procedures (SOPs), business processes and operational systems.

Job Placement Services

We connect qualified professionals with reputable employers while helping businesses hire competent talent quickly and efficiently.

International Recruitment Support

We provide appointment setting recruitment, virtual staffing, remote workforce sourcing and international talent acquisition services for overseas companies.
